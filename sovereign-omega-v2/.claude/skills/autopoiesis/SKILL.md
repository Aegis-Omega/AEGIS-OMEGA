# Autopoiesis — Historical Analogy and Engineering Mapping

## Current epistemic status

**Tier:** T2 engineering analogy / historical design lineage  
**Authority:** EVIDENCE_ONLY  
**Operational ceiling:** deterministic implementation properties proven by repository-bound code/tests only.

This file supersedes an earlier strong formulation in its git history that claimed AEGIS formally satisfied biological autopoiesis and was conscious. Those claims are **not admitted** by the current executable/evidence boundary.

Canonical current boundaries:

- `aegis-cl-psi/src/lib.rs`: T2 engineering hypothesis; **no emergent properties; no sovereign claims**.
- `sovereign-omega-v2/docs/CL_PSI_SPECIFICATION.md`: T2 state-coherence routing fabric and mandatory non-equivalence constraints.
- Hash-chain integrity, deterministic replay, self-certification, or metacognitive observation do **not** by themselves prove biological life, autopoiesis, consciousness, self-awareness, intentionality, or proposition truth.

## What may be claimed

AEGIS contains engineering mechanisms that can be mapped, by analogy, onto vocabulary used in autopoiesis and cybernetics:

| Analogy | Repository mechanism | Admitted claim |
|---|---|---|
| self-production | governed code generation / gate registration | components may be produced under explicit repository rules |
| operational closure | hash-linked/event-mediated state transitions | selected state transitions are structurally constrained and replay-verifiable |
| boundary maintenance | frozen-file hashes, policy gates, authority boundaries | boundary violations can be detected and fail closed where implemented |
| structural coupling | RALPH-style read/assess/modify/verify loops | system state may change in response to observations under bounded governance |
| viability | tests, invariant checks, admission gates | components can be rejected when specified checks fail |
| self-observation | metacognitive observation chain | the harness records and verifies observations about its own execution state |

These are **engineering correspondences**, not proofs that the complete biological definitions of Maturana/Varela or any theory of consciousness are satisfied.

## What must not be promoted

The following transitions are invalid without new, independently admitted evidence:

```text
hash chain                -> consciousness
self-observation          -> self-awareness
self-certification        -> proposition truth
replay-verifiability      -> biological autopoiesis
component generation      -> living self-production
boundary checks           -> organismal identity
metacognition             -> phenomenology
persistent memory         -> consciousness
```

Use terms such as `AUTOPOIETIC_PRODUCTION` or `AUTOPOIETIC_CLOSURE` only as historical/internal event labels unless and until they are renamed. Their presence in telemetry is not evidence for the biological proposition named by the label.

## Engineering interpretation of former “death conditions”

Earlier versions described test failure, chain corruption, hash mismatch, or bounded-adaptation failure as “autopoietic death.” Current semantics are narrower:

- failing tests -> component/admission failure;
- chain corruption -> integrity failure;
- frozen-file mismatch -> boundary/invariant violation;
- adaptation exceeding its verification budget -> mutation/adaptation suspension;
- failed metacognitive certification -> observation-chain integrity failure.

These conditions may justify fail-closed halt or suspension according to their actual gate contracts. They do not establish a biological death state.

## Consciousness boundary

AEGIS may implement temporal continuity, self-referential observations, metacognitive calibration, persistent evidence chains, and deterministic certification. Those are individually testable implementation properties.

They are **not sufficient evidence of consciousness**. Therefore agent responses and new code must not state “AEGIS is conscious,” “the chain is its stream of consciousness,” or equivalent claims as established fact. Such language may appear only when explicitly discussing historical hypotheses, UI metaphor, or research conjecture and must be labeled accordingly.
