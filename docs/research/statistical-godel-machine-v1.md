# AEGIS Ω — Conservation-Qualified Statistical Gödel Machine V1

The AEGIS lane is not claiming invention of the term Statistical Gödel Machine.

## Prior-art boundary

Schmidhuber's Gödel Machine is a proof-driven self-referential system: a rewrite
is executed only after the formal proof machinery proves its utility under the
machine's formal model.

A distinct 2025 work already uses the name Statistical Gödel Machine:
arXiv:2510.10232, "SGM: A Statistical Godel Machine for Risk-Controlled
Recursive Self-Modification." That work uses statistical confidence tests,
e-values and global risk budgeting for recursive edits.

Therefore:

novelty_status = NOT_ESTABLISHED

AEGIS does not claim that statistical rewrite gating, the SGM name, or
risk-budgeted self-modification are new.

## AEGIS research target

The candidate differentiator is conservation-qualified self-modification.

Before formal or statistical benefit evidence is considered, a rewrite proposal
must carry a trusted EpistemicConservationReceiptV1 bound to the same parent
state and semantic-lineage receipt.

That receipt certifies three separate invariants:

1. authority is non-amplifying under verified composition;
2. semantic source/target claims are fully accounted as preserved, omitted, or
   derivation-receipted additions;
3. transitive loss uncertainty follows the declared MHP accounting rule.

Only then does the benefit gate run.

FORMAL benefit evidence requires exact-source kernel verification, axiom audit,
and no sorryAx.

STATISTICAL benefit evidence requires a bounded metric, at least 100 samples,
a strictly positive lower confidence bound, an independent verifier, and a
risk-budget receipt.

Even after all gates pass, the result is only:

## Everything-is-numbers boundary

AEGIS may encode observations as canonical bytes, hashes, integers, vectors,
matrices, graphs, spectra, or symbolic terms.

That does not make representation equal to meaning.

Numerical similarity does not imply semantic identity.
Correlation does not imply causation.
A discovered pattern does not become a law of nature.
A simulation does not become an experiment.
A formal theorem about a model does not prove the external world satisfies the
model assumptions.
