# Buyer FAQ — Draft

**Do we need to adopt AEGIS?**  
No. The audit reviews one workflow in your existing stack.

**Do you need production access?**  
No. Documentation, representative evidence, and staging access may be sufficient depending on the agreed scope.

**What should we send initially?**  
A short workflow description and the highest-authority action the agent can perform. Do not send credentials, private keys, customer records, or production secrets.

**What does NOT_VERIFIED mean?**  
The available evidence is insufficient to establish either demonstrated fail-closed or demonstrated fail-open behavior.

**Is a hash chain enough?**  
No. Record integrity, action-to-approval binding, and outcome correctness are separate checks.

**Is this certification?**  
No. It is a scoped technical review and evidence package, not certification or a platform-wide safety guarantee.

**How long does it take?**  
The delivery target is five business days after written scope agreement, receipt of necessary evidence or staging access, and confirmation of an available delivery slot.

**Price?**  
USD 3,000 fixed fee for one agreed tool-using workflow.
