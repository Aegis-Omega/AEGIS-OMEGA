## 0. Purpose and evidence rule

This ledger reconstructs the repository's architectural ancestry from the first pull request through the Universal Collective Intelligence (UCI) integration spine.

GitHub issues and pull requests share one number space. Therefore a missing PR number is not evidence of missing history: some numbers are issues, dependency bots, monitoring notices, temporary reconciliation records, diagnostic PRs, or intentionally abandoned restacks.

Historical PR descriptions are evidence of what a branch claimed or attempted at that time. They are **not automatically evidence that the same property exists on current canonical `main`**. A historical test count, deployment claim, provider state, or live-service statement remains historical unless independently re-established on the current exact head.

Disposition vocabulary:

- `ABSORB` — mechanism belongs in the UCI/organism canonical spine after exact-head re-verification.
- `ACTIVE_PROOFLINE` — current separate proof lineage; do not inherit by assertion.
- `SUPERSEDED` — later work is the preferred implementation/replay of the same capability.
- `HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE` — preserve for ancestry/provenance, not current authority.
- `INTERFACE_PRODUCT` — useful product/interface surface, not authority root.
- `SECURITY_CANON` — security or fail-closed repair that constrains future UCI work.
- `REJECT_CLAIM_PRESERVE_MECHANISM` — useful code/mechanics exist, but a stronger consciousness/AGI/formal-proof claim is not established.
- `DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY` — temporary troubleshooting evidence.
- `BOT_MONITORING` — dependency/release-monitoring noise outside the organism lineage.
